Privacy Support

Support

We read every message.

Email gains@gerra.sh with what you tried, what happened, your phone model and the Gains version (TestFlight or the App Store shows it). A screenshot helps. We usually answer within a couple of days.

Importing your history

Export your workouts as a CSV file from Liftoff, Strong or Hevy, then open it with Gains or choose Import CSV in the app. Any other CSV with date, exercise, weight and reps columns works too. Importing the same history again, or an export that overlaps an earlier one, never creates duplicates.

Signing in and sync

Gains works fully without an account. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want your data backed up and synced between devices. A guest can link an account later in Settings, and what is already on the device is uploaded and merged.

Settings shows when the last sync happened and how many changes are waiting. Sync now retries straight away. If it says you were signed out on the server, sign in again with the same account: nothing is lost.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete account. Everything on our server is deleted, and the workouts on your phone stay there for you to keep using as a guest. The privacy policy has the details.

The beta

Gains is on TestFlight while we get it ready for the App Store. In TestFlight you can send feedback with a screenshot straight from the app: take a screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback.

Bugs and ideas

The code is on GitHub, and you can also report a bug or suggest something there as an issue.
