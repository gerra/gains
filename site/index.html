Your history, read back to you

Bring years of workouts with you, then let Gains point out what the numbers are saying.

  • Import everything Liftoff, Strong, Hevy or any CSV. Re-importing never duplicates.
  • Insights What's progressing, stalled, slipping or neglected, with the numbers behind each.
  • Programs GZCLP, 5/3/1 for Beginners and Reddit PPL, pre-filled from your last session.
  • Timed workouts A rest timer, and a summary with a photo when you finish.
  • Charts Estimated 1RM, weekly volume on a body map, body weight.
  • Local first Your data lives on your phone. Sign in only if you want it synced.
