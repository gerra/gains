# Findings

One report per research round, named after the run: `20261001-0200.md` for the round that
started at 02:00 UTC on 1 October 2026. The research prompt (`../prompts/research.md`) sets
the shape: a summary of at most five lines first, then the method, the themes with verbatim
quotes and their links, what changed in `../competitors.md`, and `## Backlog` with every item
added or re-scored and the reasoning. The implementation step reads the report by the item's
id, and the next round reads the two newest reports before it searches, so nothing here is
rediscovered.
